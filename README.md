# AI-Ethics-Ontology
This repostory contains a ontology of the concepts involved in the practice of ethical AI.  It can be used to clarify the concerns invoved in the deployement of AI technologies for organizations and businesses.  The framework aids in the development of oversight teams, the setting of criteria and policy for decision making on projects, and a way to respond to regulations and mitigate problems with deployment.

The ontology is developed from base ontologies of SKOS and Semantic Art's Gist.  The added classes, rules and axioms are culled from reasearching the literature.  I have used my years of experience in the field (I've been developing AI since the Expert Systems days of the '80s) and my graduate studies in Applied Ethics at Colorato State University.  

One thing I found in my research was the lack of addressing the problems of unintended effects and the unforeseen extenalities involved in the deployment of novel technologies.  I've tried to address this not by reifying the unknowns but providing a basis for questions that can generate possibilites and scenarios.  LLMs, production systems, inference and induction engines could be used to flesh these hypotheticals out.  In a sense this is constructivism and linear logic in practical application by extending the reach of resources rather than closed-world deduction from principles alone.  Not just deontological or rule-utilitarian but imaginative variation on possible worlds combined with ethical assessment.

Future and on-going work is to create an interactive interface that questions and illicit answers to generate Assessment Reports,
A JESS rule-base that interfaces with SWRL rules to test logical coherence and find aporia.  Also tools to aid in the use of the onotolgy in graph RAG large language models.  Together these could be helpful in the creation of a Sandbox to try out the ethical feasibility and viability of an AI System.

The proposed ontology is intended as a starting point and not a definitive grounding.  Of course there are going to be lacacuna and differences of emphasis on values descriptions and hierarchies. I encourage adapting this to your own needs, and am very open to discussion. Dialog has been the medium for ethics from time immemorial.  Let me know what you think!
